Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Request data deletion

Pat Guettler built Unicorn Arcade as a free app for children and families. This page explains what information the app uses and how you can control it.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves game progress locally on your device (for example, player name, coins, levels completed, and room decorations). This information is not sent to my servers and stays on the device unless you remove it.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Google services, including Google Play and (when enabled) Google AdMob for banner advertising. Those services may process device and app interaction data according to their own policies. When ads are shown, I configure child-directed, Families-appropriate advertising settings.

  • Google AdMob & privacy

Request Deletion of Your Data

Parents and guardians can delete data as follows:

  1. Game data on the device (player name, progress, coins, decorations): open your device Settings → Apps → Unicorn Arcade → Storage → Clear storage (wording may vary by device), or uninstall the app. This removes all locally saved game data.
  2. Advertising data processed by Google: Google may process data when ads are displayed. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s ad controls for options available through Google.
  3. Contact me: email grapegames3141@gmail.com with the subject “Unicorn Arcade data deletion request” and I will respond with these steps or answer questions.

I do not operate user accounts; there is no separate account to delete on my servers.

Children’s Privacy

Unicorn Arcade is designed for young children (including ages 5–8) and follows the Google Play Families Policy. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children on my servers. Local play data remains on the device as described above. When advertising is enabled, I use Google’s child-directed / Families-appropriate ad settings and do not allow behavioral advertising targeted at children in the app.

Security

Data transmitted to Google for ads or Play services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local game data on your device is protected by your device’s operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
