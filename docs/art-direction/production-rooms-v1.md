# Production rooms v1

Six new room backgrounds replace the reused React plates while preserving the original decorating-game interaction model. All final assets are 1536 x 1024 PNGs with a consistent shallow three-quarter room view, architectural detail around the perimeter, and an uninterrupted lower-floor plane for draggable decor.

## Shared generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game environment background for a mobile room-decorating game. Style/medium: premium hand-painted storybook environment art; polished gouache and watercolor texture with crisp game-ready shapes; whimsical, warm, sophisticated rather than babyish; cohesive with a magical unicorn meadow game. Composition/framing: 1536x1024 landscape, straight-on shallow three-quarter corner-room view, symmetrical and spacious, walls fill the upper 58 percent and an uninterrupted floor placement plane fills the lower 42 percent; keep the central floor open and visually calm; remain readable when cropped inside a tall phone screen. Production constraints: background plate only; no movable furniture, rugs, beds, chairs, tables, loose props, characters, unicorns, text, labels, logos or watermarks; built-in architecture at the walls and edges is allowed; avoid fisheye distortion and clutter.
