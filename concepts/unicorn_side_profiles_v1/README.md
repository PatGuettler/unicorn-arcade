# Unicorn Side-Profile Concepts v1

These six sheets are modeling references for the Unicorn Arcade cast. The supplied monochrome unicorn image is the shared proportion and silhouette anchor.

## Shared construction rules

- Strict 90-degree left-facing side profile in one neutral standing pose.
- Compact, rounded pony body; full but not overweight barrel.
- Narrow, short muzzle with a rounded forehead and large readable eye.
- Short, sturdy legs with small fitted hooves; hooves must not flare wider than the legs.
- Layered, sculptable mane and tail masses rather than loose painterly strands.
- Entire horn, ears, hooves, tail, and any wings remain inside the frame.
- Plain warm-gray studio background with soft neutral lighting.

## Character identity

- `sparkle_side_v1.png`: pearly white-lavender coat, pastel lavender/pink/cyan/gold mane and tail, gold horn, white star mark.
- `rainbow_side_v1.png`: blush-pink coat, pastel rainbow mane and tail, lilac-and-gold horn, rainbow mark.
- `star_side_v1.png`: midnight galaxy coat, blue-violet mane and tail, gold horn, white star mark.
- `cloud_side_v1.png`: pale periwinkle coat, white cloud-puff mane and tail, silver-blue horn, cloud mark.
- `dreamer_side_v1.png`: deep indigo coat with restrained gold constellation dust, navy/plum/gold mane and tail, crescent-and-stars mark.
- `mystic_side_v1.png`: opalescent lavender/cyan/pink coat, crystal horn and layered crystal wings, diamond mark.
